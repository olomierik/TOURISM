# Keyword plan — from Search Console, 14 November 2026

Source: the `sc-domain:exploretanzania.online` property, Performance report,
22 October – 10 November 2026. Every number below is from that report; none is
an estimate of search volume.

**30 clicks · ~6,000 impressions · 0.5% CTR · average position 55.6 · 809 queries**

The site is being *shown* for the right things and is on page six to nine for
nearly all of them. That is the whole finding: the job is not finding new
keywords, it is moving the ones Google already associates with the site from
position 70 to position 10. Impressions at position 80 are Google testing the
page, not people seeing it.

## Not worth chasing

`arusha` (124 impressions), `zanzibar` (121), `dar es salaam` (75),
`lake manyara` (38), `mafia island` (30). One-word place names are answered by
Wikipedia, Google Maps and the tourist board, and nobody typing them is choosing
an operator. The impressions are real and the intent is not. The phrases that
pay are the place *plus* what the visitor wants there — which is exactly what
the category × destination pages are.

## What to do, in order

1. **Put the primary phrase in each page's title and H1.** Several pages rank
   for a phrase their own title does not contain.
2. **Write the Mount Kenya hotels page properly.** Five queries, 406
   impressions, one URL, and the destination only arrived this month.
3. **Give `/trip-cost` a question-shaped H2 for each cost query** — "How much
   does a Tanzania safari cost per person?" — with the figure directly under
   it. Those are the sentences that get quoted in results.
4. **Build the Kilimanjaro operators page.** It is the only large theme with
   demand everywhere and almost no impressions here.
5. **Get listings claimed.** Operator-name queries are the ones already near
   page one, and a claimed listing with real copy is what holds the position.
6. **Publish the brand's social profiles** so `explore tanzania` resolves to
   this site.
