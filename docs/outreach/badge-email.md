# Badge invitation email (draft)

SEO brief T12. A draft for Erick to send himself, by hand or by script; nothing in the app sends it. It goes to operators whose listing is **approved and claimed**, since the badge code lives on their dashboard.

Before sending:
- Check the badge renders for that operator: `https://exploretanzania.online/badge/<their-slug>.svg`.
- "Verified" appears only on verified listings. Don't promise it to anyone else.
- The Swahili version was written for this draft and has not been checked by a native speaker. Have it read before sending.

---

## English

**Subject:** A free badge for your website, linking to your ExploreTanzania.online listing

Hello {first name},

Your listing on ExploreTanzania.online is live: {listing URL}

There is now a small badge you can add to your own website. It links your visitors to your listing, where they can read your reviews and send you an enquiry. If we have verified your business, it says "Verified on ExploreTanzania.online"; once you have three reviews, it shows your rating too.

To add it, open your dashboard, copy the code under "Add your badge to your website", and paste it into your site's footer or "About" page. There is a light version and a dark version.

Your dashboard also shows how many times the badge has been seen, and on which sites.

It is free, and you can remove it whenever you like.

If you have any questions, just reply to this email.

Erick
ExploreTanzania.online

## Kiswahili

**Kichwa:** Beji ya bure kwa tovuti yako, inayounganisha na tangazo lako kwenye ExploreTanzania.online

Habari {jina},

Tangazo lako kwenye ExploreTanzania.online liko hewani: {kiungo cha tangazo}

Sasa kuna beji ndogo unayoweza kuweka kwenye tovuti yako. Inawapeleka wageni wako kwenye tangazo lako, ambapo wanaweza kusoma maoni ya wateja na kukutumia ombi la safari. Kama tumeithibitisha biashara yako, inaandika "Verified on ExploreTanzania.online"; ukipata maoni matatu, itaonyesha pia alama zako.

Ili kuiweka, fungua dashibodi yako, nakili msimbo ulio chini ya "Add your badge to your website", kisha ubandike kwenye sehemu ya chini ya tovuti yako au kwenye ukurasa wa "Kuhusu sisi". Kuna toleo jeupe na toleo jeusi.

Dashibodi yako pia inaonyesha beji imeonekana mara ngapi, na kwenye tovuti zipi.

Ni bure, na unaweza kuiondoa wakati wowote.

Ukiwa na swali lolote, jibu barua pepe hii tu.

Erick
ExploreTanzania.online
