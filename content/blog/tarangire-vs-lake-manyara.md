Both parks sit an easy drive from Arusha, both are usually the first stop on a northern-circuit safari, and many itineraries have room for only one. The short version: **Tarangire for elephants in the dry season, Lake Manyara for forest, birds and a shorter day.**

## At a glance

| | Tarangire | Lake Manyara |
|---|---|---|
| Known for | Baobabs and huge dry-season elephant herds | Groundwater forest, soda lake, tree-climbing lions |
| Best months | June to October, best in September and October | July to October for game; November to June for birds |
| Peak wildlife rating (our park pages) | 5/5 in July, August and September | 4/5 from June to October |
| Low season | December to April (wildlife 2/5) | Wildlife stays at 3/5 through the rains |
| Mid-range cost per person per day | $230–400 | $220–380 |
| Park fees in that | $80–130 | $70–120 |

## Tarangire: go late in the dry season

Tarangire's wildlife depends on its river. By September and October, the river is the only water for miles and game concentrates along it, which is when the park is at its best. From November to May it turns green and is excellent for birds, but the animals disperse and sightings take work.

It is also the quietest of the northern circuit's big parks.

## Lake Manyara: shorter, greener, steadier

Manyara is a narrow strip between the Rift Valley escarpment and a soda lake. July to October is dry, with good game along the shoreline. November to June is greener, better for birds and for flamingos on the lake, and the forest is at its best.

The lake level swings a great deal between years, and in wet years much of the floodplain is under water.

Because its ratings stay steady through the year, Manyara is the safer pick for a trip in the rains.

## Which to choose

- **Travelling June to October with one day to spare:** Tarangire.
- **Travelling in the green season, or a keen birder:** Lake Manyara.
- **Only a half day before driving on to Ngorongoro:** Lake Manyara is the shorter detour.
- **Two days:** do both. Many operators combine them.
