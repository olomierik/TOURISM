## Self-drive or with a driver?

**With a driver** is the default for safaris. The driver knows the park roads, the rules on where vehicles may go, and where animals have been seen that week. You also avoid the paperwork of taking a hire car through park gates.

**Self-drive** suits travellers on the coast, in towns, or on a slow overland trip, and is cheaper per day. Rental companies generally ask for:

- your national driving licence and an **International Driving Permit**;
- a credit card for the deposit;
- confidence driving on the **left**.

## 4x4 rules in the parks

National parks expect a high-clearance 4x4, and in the rains the roads in Tarangire, Serengeti and Ngorongoro become impassable without one. Vehicles pay their own park entry fee on top of each person's fee, and foreign-registered vehicles are charged differently from Tanzanian ones, so ask the rental company what the vehicle fee will be.

Ngorongoro also charges a crater descent fee per vehicle, **US$295** on the 2026 tariff this site uses.

## Prices

Most companies quote by the day, with or without a driver, and fuel is usually extra. Few companies on this site have published a day rate yet, so the fastest way to compare is to ask several at once.

## What to check before you sign

- Is fuel included, and is the car returned full or empty?
- What does the insurance cover, and what is the excess?
- Is there a mileage limit?
- For safaris: does the price include the driver's park fees and food?
- Is a pop-up roof included? It is what makes game viewing comfortable.
