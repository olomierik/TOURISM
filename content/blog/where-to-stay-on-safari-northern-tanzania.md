Where you sleep on safari decides more than the room. It decides how far you drive before the first game drive, whether you are near the herds in the month you travel, and a large part of what the trip costs. This guide goes park by park through Tanzania's northern circuit and answers the question that matters most: **which area to stay in**. Then it names lodges and camps listed on this site in each one.

None of the places named here has a traveller review on this site yet, so this is a guide to where to look, not a ranking. Every place has its own page with its location and contact details, and you can ask it, or an operator, directly.

## The short answer

- **Serengeti:** stay where the herds are in your month. That's the south (Ndutu) from about December to March, the centre (Seronera) all year, the west (Grumeti) around June and July, and the north (Kogatende and Lamai) from about July to October.
- **Ngorongoro Crater:** stay on the **crater rim** to be on the crater floor early, or in **Karatu** to pay less and accept a longer drive in the morning.
- **Tarangire:** stay **inside the park** in the dry season, when the game gathers along the river; camps just outside cost less.
- **Lake Manyara:** most lodges sit on the **escarpment above the lake**, near Mto wa Mbu, which also makes them a convenient first or last night of a northern circuit trip.

## Lodge, tented camp or campsite

Safari accommodation in Tanzania comes in four broad kinds:

- **Lodges** are permanent buildings with solid walls, a restaurant and often a pool. They tend to be larger and sit in the busier, central parts of the parks.
- **Tented camps** are canvas rooms on fixed platforms, usually with a proper bed and an en-suite bathroom. Many are small, and some are as expensive as any lodge.
- **Mobile camps** are tented camps that move during the year to stay near the wildebeest migration. They are the way to sleep close to the herds, wherever they are.
- **Public campsites** are run by the parks. They are the cheapest way to stay inside a park, and you bring (or your operator brings) the tents, the cook and the food.

Which one you choose is the biggest single driver of cost after the park fees. On the cost bands this site publishes, a day in the Serengeti runs about **US$180–250 per person on a budget, US$250–450 mid-range, and US$700–1,500 at the luxury end**. The park fees inside those figures, US$100–170, are the same whichever bed you choose. The [Safari Price Index](/safari-price-index) has the same breakdown for every park.

## Serengeti: choose the area before the lodge

The Serengeti is big enough that "a lodge in the Serengeti" can mean a five-hour drive from where the animals are. The resident wildlife (lions, leopards, elephants, giraffes) is there all year. The wildebeest migration moves in a rough loop that follows the rain, so the right area depends on your month. The timings below are typical, not guaranteed; a late or early rainy season shifts them by weeks.

### Central Serengeti (Seronera): any month

Seronera, in the middle of the park, has year-round game and the densest network of roads and lodges. It is the safe choice if you are not chasing the migration, or if you have only two nights. It is also where the park's public campsites are.

Listed around Seronera and the central Serengeti: [Seronera Wildlife Lodge](/business/seronera-wildlife-lodge) and [Serengeti Serena Safari Lodge](/business/serengeti-serena-safari-lodge).

### Southern Serengeti and Ndutu: roughly December to March

In the green season the herds come south to the short-grass plains to give birth, and the calving draws predators. Much of this area, around Lake Ndutu and Lake Masek, sits just inside the Ngorongoro Conservation Area rather than the national park, so it falls under the conservation area's fees and rules.

Listed in the south: [Ndutu Safari Lodge](/business/ndutu-safari-lodge), [Lake Masek Tented Lodge](/business/lake-masek-tented-lodge) and [Kusini Serengeti Camp](/business/kusini-serengeti-camp).

### Western corridor (Grumeti): roughly May to July

As the plains dry out, the herds move west and north-west towards the Grumeti River. This part of the park is quieter, with fewer vehicles than Seronera.

Listed in the west: [Grumeti Serengeti River Lodge](/business/grumeti-serengeti-river-lodge) and [Mbalageti Serengeti](/business/mbalageti-serengeti).

### Northern Serengeti (Kogatende and Lamai): roughly July to October

This is the season of the Mara River crossings, the scenes most people picture when they think of the migration. The north is remote, and many visitors fly in to a nearby airstrip rather than drive.

Listed in the north: [Serengeti Ark Safari Lodge, Kogatende](/business/serengeti-ark-safari-lodge-kogatende), [Nomad Tanzania Lamai](/business/nomad-tanzania-lamai-serengeti) and, further east, [Lobo Wildlife Lodge](/business/lobo-wildlife-lodge).

### How many nights?

Two nights is the minimum that gives you a full day in the park, because the drive in from Ngorongoro takes most of a day by the time you have stopped for game on the way. Three or four nights let you split them between two areas. The site lists nearly 200 places to stay in and around the Serengeti; see [all of them on a map](/hotels/serengeti).

## Ngorongoro Crater: rim or Karatu

Nobody sleeps in the crater itself: there is no accommodation on the crater floor, and you visit it on a day drive down the descent road and back up. So the choice is between the rim and the farmland towns below it.

### On the crater rim

The rim lodges sit at over 2,000 metres, with views straight into the crater. Their advantage is time: you can be on the descent road early, when the light is good and before the vehicles from further away arrive. The rim is cold at night all year, so bring a warm layer even in the dry season.

The crater descent is charged per vehicle on top of each person's fees (US$295 on the 2026 tariff our park pages use). Most visits are a single day on the floor, so one night on the rim is the usual pattern.

Listed on or near the rim: [Ngorongoro Wildlife Lodge](/business/ngorongoro-wildlife-lodge), [Lemala Ngorongoro Tented Camp](/business/lemala-ngorongoro-tented-camp) and, on the north-western side, [Entamanu Ngorongoro](/business/nomad-tanzania-entamanu-ngorongoro).

### Karatu and the highlands below

Karatu is a farming town on the road between Lake Manyara and the crater, surrounded by coffee estates. Lodges here cost less than the rim, many have gardens and space to walk, and they work well as a base for both the crater and Lake Manyara. The cost is the morning drive: roughly an hour up to the descent road, and the same back at the end of the day.

Listed around Karatu: [Gibb's Farm](/business/gibb-s-farm), [The Plantation Lodge](/business/the-plantation-lodge), [The Manor at Ngorongoro](/business/the-manor-at-ngorongoro) and [Rhotia Valley Lodge](/business/rhotia-valley-lodge).

There are over 100 places listed in the Ngorongoro area: [see them all](/hotels/ngorongoro).

## Tarangire: inside the park in the dry season

[Tarangire](/destinations/tarangire-national-park) is at its best from about July to October, when its river is the only water for miles and elephants and other game gather along it. In those months, staying inside the park puts you next to the action at dawn and dusk, when game drives are best. In the green season (roughly November to May) the animals spread out, and a cheaper camp outside the gate loses you little.

Listed inside the park: [Tarangire Sopa Lodge](/business/tarangire-sopa-lodge), [Maweninga Camp](/business/maweninga-camp-tarangire-national-park), [Kuro Tarangire](/business/nomad-tanzania-kuro-tarangire), [Oliver's Camp](/business/oliver-s-camp) and [Sanctuary Swala](/business/sanctuary-swala). Oliver's, Kuro and Swala are in the quieter south-east of the park, a longer drive from the main gate.

Listed just outside, to the east: [Tarangire Treetops](/business/tarangire-treetops) and [Kikoti Tented Camp](/business/kikoti-tented-camp).

One night is enough for many itineraries. Two let you reach the southern part of the park, which sees far fewer vehicles. [All places to stay around Tarangire](/hotels/tarangire-national-park).

## Lake Manyara: the escarpment above the lake

[Lake Manyara](/destinations/lake-manyara-national-park) is a narrow park between the Rift Valley wall and a soda lake. Most of its lodges sit on top of the escarpment above the village of Mto wa Mbu, looking down over the forest and the lake. Because Manyara sits on the road from Arusha to Ngorongoro and the Serengeti, these lodges are often used as a first night out of Arusha, or a last night on the way back, as much as for the park itself.

Listed on the escarpment: [Lake Manyara Serena Safari Lodge](/business/lake-manyara-serena-safari-lodge), [Kirurumu Manyara Lodge](/business/kirurumu-manyara-lodge) and [Escarpment Luxury Lodge](/business/escarpment-luxury-lodge).

Inside the park, in the south: [&Beyond Lake Manyara Tree Lodge](/business/and-beyond-lake-manyara-tree-lodge). It is far from the gate, which is the point: you have the southern part of the park largely to yourself.

The Karatu lodges above are also within reach of Manyara, so one base can cover both the lake and the crater. [All places to stay around Lake Manyara](/hotels/lake-manyara-national-park).

## A sample week, and where to sleep

A common seven-day northern circuit, with one way to place the nights:

- **Night 1:** Lake Manyara escarpment or Karatu, after a game drive in Tarangire or Manyara.
- **Night 2:** Tarangire, inside the park in the dry season.
- **Nights 3–5:** Serengeti, in the area that matches your month (two nights in one area and one in another, if it suits the season).
- **Night 6:** Ngorongoro crater rim, for an early descent the next morning.
- **Day 7:** crater floor, then back to Arusha.

Operators rearrange this all the time, and they will know which camps have space in your dates. The tours operators publish on this site show what such a week costs. The [7-Day Tailored Northern Circuit Safari](/packages/7-day-tailored-northern-circuit-safari-2ef33e) was listed at US$3,295 per person on 21 November 2026.

## Questions people ask

**Can you stay inside Ngorongoro Crater?** No. There is no accommodation on the crater floor. You stay on the rim or below it in Karatu, and drive down for the day.

**Is it better to stay inside or outside the Serengeti?** For the Serengeti, inside: the park is so large that a base outside the gates means hours of driving each day. For Tarangire and Lake Manyara, outside is a sensible way to save money, especially in the green season.

**Where should I stay to see the Great Migration?** Wherever the herds are in your month: the south around Ndutu from about December to March, the west around May to July, the north from about July to October. Mobile camps follow the herds, so ask operators which camps will be where on your dates.

**Do I need to book lodges myself?** Most travellers book their safari through a local operator, who books the lodges, the vehicle and the park fees together, and can often get lodge rates you cannot. You can also contact any of the places above directly.
